Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-07

Riverwood Drive Indexer ("the app") is an internal tool built for a single organization's own use. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that data is used.

What we access

The app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. Under this scope, the app can only access files and folders that the account holder explicitly selects via the Google Picker at the time of authorization. The app cannot browse, list, or read any other file in the account holder's Google Drive.

How the data is used

Files and folders the account holder selects are periodically scanned. Extracted text (from documents) and transcripts (from audio recordings) are stored in a private index used solely to answer questions from members of the same organization. No data is shared with any third party, sold, or used for advertising.

Data retention

Indexed content is retained until the organization removes it or revokes the app's access. Access can be revoked at any time via Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions about this app can be directed to the organization that operates it (AINativeAgentManager / riverwood project).
