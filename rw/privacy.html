Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

rw Drive Indexer ("the app") is an internal tool built for a single organization's own use, operated with the explicit consent of the single Google account holder whose Drive it reads. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that data is used.

What we access

The app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly scopes against the single Google account that authorized it. Under these scopes the app can list and read the content of files and folders in that account's Google Drive. The app cannot write, modify, or delete anything in Drive, and it never accesses any Google account other than the one that explicitly authorized it.

How the data is used

Files and folders in the authorized account are periodically scanned. Extracted text (from documents) and transcripts (from audio recordings) are stored in a private index used solely to answer questions from members of the same organization. No data is shared with any third party, sold, or used for advertising.

Data retention

Indexed content is retained until the organization removes it or revokes the app's access. Access can be revoked at any time via Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions about this app can be directed to the organization that operates it (AINativeAgentManager / rw project).
